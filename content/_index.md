Welcome to the docs for the Fantasy Football Legacy League (FFLegacy). Here you can
find the rules for the league and other useful tools to help you build and coach
a winning team this season.

## Sleeper

We are using Sleeper to manage our league. You can find our league at the
following URL: https://sleeper.com/leagues/1392749078860820480

While Sleeper already has details on many of the rules in this rulebook, we keep
a copy here written in plain English so everyone has a firm understanding of
what's going on.
